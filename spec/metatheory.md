# Formal Metatheory & Open Proof-Theoretic Boundaries (`UL-Core`)

This document formalizes the metatheoretic invariants of [core.md](core.md) (`v0.3.0`) and [worked-examples.md](worked-examples.md), and labels each result on the claim status ladder of [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) §2 item 5: a `Tested` result is exercised by bound tests but not proved, a `Decided` result is ratified but not yet met by the reference kernel, and an `Exploratory` result is open.

MT-9 is `Proved`: the maintainer approved its written proof on 2026-11-26 and, the same day, approved its revision for the closure conditions of [`audit-universal-existential-import`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-universal-existential-import). No other result is `Proved`; the proof sketches elsewhere are sketches, not proofs.

## 1. Well-Foundedness & Fixed-Point Semantics

1. `Tested` **MT-1: Unique Well-Founded Authority and Anti-Circularity** ([core.md](core.md) §3 items 8 and 10, §5 item 14; [`audit-spec-kernel-coupling`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-spec-kernel-coupling)):
   - **Kernel**: the reference kernel computes the well-founded model of `Π(Σ)`, and a differential suite compares it with a clean-room oracle written from item 14 alone; non-normative notes are in the [metatheory-to-kernel map](../README.md#metatheory-to-kernel-map).
   - **Statement**: For every inquiry state `Σ` and finite query set `Q`:
     1. `Π(Σ)` has exactly one well-founded model `M`, computable by the alternating fixpoint in time polynomial in the size of its ground instantiation.
     2. **Anti-circularity**: a record supported only through mutual-support cycles, with no derivation from base observations, recorded theorems or structural axioms, is false in `M`.
     3. **Collision-free states**: if no `coll` atom is true or undefined in `M` (in particular under `No-Gluts`), then `M` is two-valued, every `obs` atom is false, and `M` coincides with the least model of the program obtained by deleting every `not obs(...)` literal.
   - **Proof sketch**: `Π(Σ)` is a finite ground normal logic program (item 14, Universe), and negation occurs only through `obs`. Part 1 is the existence and uniqueness of the well-founded model of a normal program and its alternating-fixpoint characterization (Van Gelder, Ross and Schlipf, 1991). Part 2: the atoms of such a cycle form an unfounded set, and unfounded sets are false in the well-founded model. Part 3: `obs` atoms are derived only from `coll` atoms, so if every `coll` atom is false, every `not obs(...)` literal is true in `M`, and the remaining program is definite, whose well-founded model is its least model.

## 2. Self-Reference & Triviality Resistance

2. `Tested` **MT-2: Curry Firewall Soundness (Level Stratification & Mutual Strict Positivity)** ([core.md](core.md) §1 item 3; [worked-examples.md](worked-examples.md) §3; [`layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier`](../docs/design-review.md#layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier)):
   - **Statement**: Suppose every recursive item/boundary definition system `{X_i := F_i(X⃗)}` obeys:
     1. **Level Stratification**: Every reflection item `Refl_k(J)` occurring inside a level-`n` definition satisfies `level(J) ≤ k < n`; and
     2. **Mutual Strict Positivity**: In the directed dependency graph over `{X_i}`, no directed cycle traverses a **contravariant edge**—namely, passing to the left of `→`, inside an unguarded strong polarity flip `~` (that feeds a conditional or negative position), or inside a boundary promotion `[δ]` / quantifier domain constraint `D`.
     Then every admissible definition admits a well-founded denotational interpretation across the stratified hierarchy `M₀ ⊆ M₁ ⊆ ... ⊆ M_ω`, blocking Curry's Paradox (`⊢ Z` for arbitrary `Z`) across all four known attack vectors (`K := K → Z`, same-level `Refl` diagonal, `K := (~K) → Z`, and boundary-promotion `K := ∀(y : B ⊓ [K]) Z`).
   - **Proof Sketch**: Proceed by lexicographic induction on `(n, structural-depth)`:
     - Fix all evaluations `⊩⁺_k, ⊩⁻_k` at levels `k < n` (`M_{<n}`). Any `Refl_k(J)` with `k < n` acts as a fixed atomic predicate in `M_{<n}` whose positive/negative witness sets cannot shift while solving level `n`.
     - Within level `n`, contract non-contravariant DAG edges into finite strictly positive monotone operators over `(𝒫(Pos_n), 𝒫(Neg_n))` (note that `∧`, `∨`, right-hand side of `→`, and quantifier bodies preserve positivity). By Knaster–Tarski, each level-`n` recursive component admits a unique least fixpoint.
     - Vector 1 (`left-of-implies`), Vector 3 (`~` feeding `→`), and Vector 4 (`boundary-promotion`) each induce a self-loop with at least one contravariant edge and are syntactically rejected at formation time. Vector 2 fails same-level stratification (`n < n` impossible); well-typed cross-stage reflection `K_{t+1} := Refl_t(Σ_t ; B ⊩⁺ K_t) → Z` separates `K_t` from `K_{t+1}`, breaking Step 2 (`K ⊢ K → Z`) of the Curry derivation.
   - **Kernel**: non-normative implementation notes are in the [metatheory-to-kernel map](../README.md#metatheory-to-kernel-map).

3. `Tested` **MT-3: Non-Explosion & Non-Witnessing of `⊥` on Non-Empty Boundaries** ([core.md](core.md) §1 item 1, §2 item 4, §3 items 10–12; [`layer1-collision-quarantine`](../docs/design-review.md#layer1-collision-quarantine)):
   - **Statement**:
     1. **Global Non-Explosion**: If `(B, A)` enters `Collision` in state `Σ`, arbitrary unrelated items `Z` are **not** derivable (`B ⊩⁺ Z` does not follow from `Collision` on `(B, A)` alone).
     2. **Non-Witnessing of `⊥` (`Live` Safety)**: Under base admissibility (`⊥ ∉ G⁺`), no non-empty boundary `B ≠ ⊥_B` ever holds a `Live` positive witness `Σ ; B ⊩⁺ ⊥ [π]`.
   - **Proof Sketch**, over the evaluation program `Π(Σ)` of [core.md](core.md) §5 item 14:
     - Part 1: `coll` and `obs` atoms occur in rule bodies only as `not obs(...)` literals of `live` clauses; no `rec` clause has them in its body, and no rule of the item 14 table takes both `(B, +, A)` and `(B, -, A)` as premises. A collision therefore never supplies a premise for any record: it can only make an `obs` atom true or undefined and so withhold authority. Every record is derived from base observations, recorded theorems and structural axioms by the rules of the table, whether or not any collision occurs.
     - Part 2: `rec(B, -, ⊥)` is a structural fact, true on every `B ∈ 𝔅(Σ)`. A `live` atom that is true in the well-founded model `M` comes from a rule instance whose premises are all `Live`, hence all recorded, so its own `rec` atom is true as well. If `live(B, +, ⊥)` were true, `rec(B, +, ⊥)` would be true, hence `coll(B, ⊥)` and `obs(B, ⊥)`, and the `not obs(B, ⊥)` literal of every `live(B, +, ⊥)` clause would be false: a contradiction. So `live(B, +, ⊥)` is false or undefined, whichever rule would produce `+⊥`. Two cases illustrate this. Under a structural collapse `B ⊓ [A] = ⊥_B`, `-A` is structural, an observed `+A` collides definitely and is never `Live`, Scoped Cut cannot fire, and `rec(B, +, ⊥)` is false. Under observed `+A` and `+(A → ⊥)`, `imposs-excl` and the counterexample rule form a symmetric negative cycle, so `rec(B, +, ⊥)` and `coll(B, ⊥)` are undefined and `(B, ⊥)` reads `Collision` (G4b) with no `+⊥` record present.
   - **Kernel**: non-normative implementation notes are in the [metatheory-to-kernel map](../README.md#metatheory-to-kernel-map).

## 3. Boundary Algebra, Disjoint Isolation & Cover Stability

4. `Tested` **MT-4: Disjoint Regime Zero-Leakage & Selective Quarantine Survival** ([core.md](core.md) §2 item 4, §3 items 9–10; [worked-examples.md](worked-examples.md) §1):
   - **Statement**: Suppose `B₁, B₂ ≠ ⊥_B` are disjoint (`B₁ ⊓ B₂ = ⊥_B`): disjointness is declared for them or inherited from declared ancestors ([core.md](core.md) §2 item 4); an unknown overlap does not qualify. Suppose further that `Σ₁` extends `Σ₀` solely by adding a counterexample `Observe⁻(B₂, A, w)` (or derived exclusions native to `B₂`). Then:
     1. **Zero Negative Leakage**: For every non-empty sub-boundary `B' ⊑ B₁`, the exclusion `B₂ ⊩⁻ A` cannot inherit onto `B'`.
     2. **Local Non-Obstruction**: `(B₁, A)` itself is not obstructed by `Collision` on `(B₂, A)`.
     3. **Selective Survival**: Every ground `(B₁, ±Y [π])` that was `Live` in `Σ₀` and whose provenance DAG `π` contains no node `(B_sup, X)` with `B₂ ⊑ B_sup` remains `Live` in `Σ₁`.
   - **Proof Sketch**:
     - For (1), downward heredity transfers entries only to non-empty `B' ⊑ B₂`. Any `B'` with both `B' ⊑ B₁` and `B' ⊑ B₂` satisfies `B' ⊑ B₁ ⊓ B₂ = ⊥_B`, contradicting `B' ≠ ⊥_B`.
     - For (2), obstruction propagates strictly upward along `⊑` (a collision on `B_col` obstructs `(B_ext, A)` iff `B_col ⊑ B_ext`). Since `B₂ ⊑ B₁` would yield `⊥_B = B₁ ⊓ B₂ = B₂ ≠ ⊥_B` (contradiction), `B₂ ⋢ B₁`.
     - For (3), any node `(B_node, X)` in `π` with `B₂ ⋢ B_node` cannot become `obstructed` by any collision at or below `B₂`; by structural induction over the acyclic DAG `π`, every dependency in `π` remains in `Live(Σ₁)`, so `(B₁, ±Y [π]) ∈ Live(Σ₁)`.

5. `Tested` **MT-5: Meet-Stability of Cover-Witnessed Disjunction (`D2`) & Soundness of `∨E` Gluing** ([core.md](core.md) §2 item 5, §3 item 12; [`layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers`](../docs/design-review.md#layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers)):
   - **Statement**:
     1. **Downward Heredity of `D2` Disjunction**: If `Σ ; B ⊩⁺ (A ∨ C) [π]` is grounded by a witnessed exhaustive cover `{B₁, ..., B_k} ▷ B`, then for every non-empty sub-boundary `B' ⊑ B` whose overlap with each cell is known (overlapping or disjoint, [core.md](core.md) §2 item 4), `{B_i ⊓ B' | B_i known to overlap B'} ▷ B'` witnesses `Σ ; B' ⊩⁺ (A ∨ C)`.
     2. **Soundness of Cover Gluing (`∨E`)**: From `Live` `B ⊩⁺ (A ∨ C)` (via `{B_i} ▷ B`), `Live` `B ⊩⁺ (A → E)`, and `Live` `B ⊩⁺ (C → E)`, every cell `B_i` acquires a `Live` witness `B_i ⊩⁺ E`, justifying `B ⊩⁺ E`.
   - **Proof Sketch**:
     - By Axiom 3 (Meet-Stability, [core.md](core.md) §2 item 5), `{B_i ⊓ B' | B_i known to overlap B'}` is a witnessed exhaustive cover of `B'`. Validity (item 5) excludes a registered cover with a non-empty sub-boundary disjoint from every cell, so, with every overlap of `B'` known, this cover contains at least one non-empty cell `B_j ⊓ B'`. On each such cell `B_j ⊓ B' ⊑ B_j`, downward heredity (`§3 item 9`) inherits whichever of `B_j ⊩⁺ A` or `B_j ⊩⁺ C` held on `B_j`.
     - For `∨E`, downward heredity restricts both `B ⊩⁺ (A → E)` and `B ⊩⁺ (C → E)` to every cell `B_i ⊑ B`. Since each non-empty `B_i` has a `Live` witness for either `A` or `C`, `Scoped Cut` (`B_i ⊓ B_i = B_i ≠ ⊥_B`) produces `B_i ⊩⁺ E` on every cell of `{B_i} ▷ B`. Gluing across the exhaustive cover `{B_i} ▷ B` grounds `B ⊩⁺ E`.
   - **Kernel**: non-normative implementation notes are in the [metatheory-to-kernel map](../README.md#metatheory-to-kernel-map).

## 4. Inquiry Dynamics & Anti-Immunization

6. `Tested` **MT-6: Polarity Purity of `Retract` and `Withdraw`, Reinstatement & Vertical Non-Commutativity** ([core.md](core.md) §2 item 7; [`layer4-negation-vs-exclusion`](../docs/design-review.md#layer4-negation-vs-exclusion)):
   - **Statement**:
     1. **Pure Cancellation (Sanskrit *prasajya-pratiṣedha*)**: Applying `Retract(g)` strictly deletes `g` from `G⁺ ∪ G⁻` without adding any element to either `G⁺` or `G⁻`. Likewise, `Withdraw(c)` strictly deletes the constraint `c` from `C_B` without adding any element to `G⁺`, `G⁻` or `C_B`. In particular, if `(B, A)` was `Grounded` (resp. `Excluded`) in `Σ` solely via a unique base leaf `g`, then in `Retract(g)(Σ)` the status of `(B, A)` becomes `Open`, never `Excluded` (resp. `Grounded`).
     2. **Quarantine Reinstatement**: If `(B, A)` was in `Collision` between `+A [g⁺]` and `-A [g⁻]`, then `Retract(g⁻)` removes the collision and restores `Live` authority to `+A [g⁺]` and any downstream derivations depending solely on `g⁺` and other `Live` leaves (without adding new positive leaves).
     3. **Vertical Non-Commutativity**: Vertical transition sequences `Σ₀ → Σ₁ → Σ₂` violate `Exchange` (`Observe⁺(B, A, w); Retract(w)` differs from `Retract(w); Observe⁺(B, A, w)`).
   - **Proof Sketch**: Immediate from the state transition definition (`§2 item 7`): `Retract(g)` modifies `Σ = (G⁺, G⁻, C_B, O_rev)` solely by set difference on `G⁺ ∪ G⁻` and re-evaluation of derived provenance. Since every derived entry for `±A` requires at least one base leaf in `G⁺ ∪ G⁻` (or structural `C_B`) by MT-1, deleting the sole base leaf eliminates all derived entries on that polarity without constructing an opposite-polarity leaf. `Withdraw(c)` modifies `Σ` solely by set difference on `C_B`; every rule instance that a constraint supports names it in its side condition (Structural, Incompatibility, `∨` by cover, Cover-Gluing, `∀` generic, `∃` exclusion; [core.md](core.md) §5 item 14), so removing `c` removes those instances and adds none. Counterexample for (3): starting from `Open` `(B, A)` with `w ∉ G⁺`, `Observe⁺(B, A, w); Retract(w)` ends in `Open`, whereas `Retract(w); Observe⁺(B, A, w)` leaves `(B, A)` `Grounded`.

## 5. Four-Tier Specialization & Finite-Domain Classical Valuation

8. `Tested` **MT-8a: Constructive Specializations — No Quarantine Under `No-Gluts` and the Rule Sets of `NJ_min` and `NJ`** ([core.md](core.md) §3 item 12, §4 item 13; [`layer2-classical-boundary-recovery`](../docs/design-review.md#layer2-classical-boundary-recovery); [`audit-metatheorem-overclaim`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-metatheorem-overclaim)):

10. `Proved` **MT-9: `UL-Classical` Finite-Domain Bivalent Valuation (`2` on an Atomic Cell, `2^k` on a Finite Atomic Cover)** ([core.md](core.md) §4 item 13; [worked-examples.md](worked-examples.md) §4; [`scope-anchor-unified-core`](../docs/design-review.md#scope-anchor-unified-core); [`audit-metatheorem-overclaim`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-metatheorem-overclaim)). Proof: the written proof below, reviewed and approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-26, revision included.
    - **Statement**: Assume the `UL-Classical` closure conditions (C1)–(C7) of [core.md](core.md) §4 item 13, and consider closed level-`0` items that mention only handles of `Dom(B*)`.
      1. **Atomic cell (`2`)**: On a non-empty atomic cell `B*`, setting `v(A) := 1` when `B* ⊩⁺ A` and `v(A) := 0` when `B* ⊩⁻ A` defines a total function on these items. It satisfies `v(⊥) = 0`, `v(~A) = 1 − v(A)`, `v(A ∧ C) = min(v(A), v(C))`, `v(A ∨ C) = max(v(A), v(C))`, `v(A → C) = max(1 − v(A), v(C))`, `v(∀(x : D) A(x)) = min_{w ∈ D_δ} v(A(w))` and `v(∃(x : D) A(x)) = max_{w ∈ D_δ} v(A(w))`, with `D_δ` as in (C7), `min ∅ = 1` and `max ∅ = 0`, and whenever `v(t ≈_{B*} u) = 1`, `v(R(…, r: t, …)) = v(R(…, r: u, …))` for every relation `R` native to `B*`. It is the only function with these clauses that agrees with the atoms and congruence items. Consequently `~A` and `A → ⊥` take the same value, `v(A ∨ ~A) = 1`, and no `UL-Classical` state satisfies both `+A` and `-A`, so `A, ~A ⊨_{B*} C` holds vacuously.
      2. **Finite atomic cover (`2^k`)**: Let `B ≠ ⊥_B` and let `{B*₁, …, B*_k} ▷ B` (`k ≥ 1`) be a witnessed cover by distinct atomic cells, each satisfying (C3)–(C7), with valuations `v₁, …, v_k`. Then `B ⊩⁺ A` iff `vᵢ(A) = 1` for every `i`, `B ⊩⁻ A` iff `vᵢ(A) = 0` for every `i`, and `A` is `split` otherwise; every classical tautology holds globally on `B` ([worked-examples.md](worked-examples.md) Derivation 4A).
      3. **Scope**: under (C4), the conditions (C1)–(C7) determine a unique classical valuation of a finite structure. MT-9 does not show that (C1)–(C7) can hold together, that is, that the closure rules (C6) and (C7) never force a `Collision` with the rules of [core.md](core.md) §3; that existence result is MT-9b. Validity over finite structures differs from first-order validity (Trakhtenbrot's theorem), so MT-9 does not derive classical first-order consequence from `UL-Core`.
    - **Proof:** (written for v0.3.0, approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-26, and revised and re-approved the same day when (C8) was withdrawn.)
      - *Authority*: By (C4) no `(B, X)` is in `Collision` on any boundary of `Σ`, so no ground is obstructed and every derivable ground is `Live`. This holds under the v0.2.0 reading of [core.md](core.md) §3 item 10 and under the well-founded evaluation ratified in [`audit-spec-kernel-coupling`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-spec-kernel-coupling): there `Collision` is reported when the collision atom is true or undefined, so (C4) makes every collision atom false, obstruction never holds, the evaluation program has no effective negation, and its well-founded model is its least model, with no ground left `Undetermined`.
      - *Bivalence and clauses*, by structural induction on a closed level-`0` item `A` on `B*`; a constraint `δ` counts as a component of its quantifier, so each `δ(w)` falls under the induction hypothesis. In each case the induction hypothesis fixes the polarities of the components, an introduction rule of [core.md](core.md) §3 (or a closure rule of §4 item 13) derives the polarity the clause predicts, and (C4) excludes the opposite polarity. Only the introduction direction (`⟸`) of each clause of §3 item 12 is used.
        - Atoms and congruence items: (C5), since their handles lie in `Dom(B*)`.
        - `⊥`: `B* ⊩⁻ ⊥` holds structurally (§3 item 12), so `v(⊥) = 0`.
        - `~A`: if `v(A) = 0`, then `-A` gives `+(~A)`; if `v(A) = 1`, then `+A` gives `-(~A)` (§3 item 12). Hence `v(~A) = 1 − v(A)`.
        - `A ∧ C`: if `v(A) = v(C) = 1`, then `+A` and `+C` give `+(A ∧ C)`; otherwise `-A` or `-C` gives `-(A ∧ C)`. Hence `min`.
        - `A ∨ C`: if `v(A) = 1` or `v(C) = 1`, the identity cover `{B*} ▷ B*` (Axiom 2) witnesses `+(A ∨ C)`; otherwise `-A` and `-C` give `-(A ∨ C)`. Hence `max`.
        - `A → C`: (C6) fixes the polarity, `-` exactly when `v(A) = 1` and `v(C) = 0` and `+` otherwise. Hence `max(1 − v(A), v(C))`.
        - `∀(x : D) A(x)` and `∃(x : D) A(x)`: by (C3), `Dom(D) ⊆ Dom(B*)` is finite and closed, so each instance `A(w)` and each `δ(w)` mentions only handles of `Dom(B*)` and is bivalent by the induction hypothesis. This fixes `D_δ`. Exactly one side of each clause of (C7) then holds, giving `min` and `max` over `D_δ`; for an empty `D_δ` only `+∀` and `-∃` hold, matching `min ∅ = 1` and `max ∅ = 0` and the vacuous grounding of item 12 on a closed domain.
        - Congruence: if `v(t ≈_{B*} u) = 1`, substitutivity (§2 item 6), which applies to both polarities, carries `+R(…, r: t, …)` to `+R(…, r: u, …)` and `-R(…, r: t, …)` to `-R(…, r: u, …)`. Both instances are bivalent, so `v(R(…, r: u, …)) = v(R(…, r: t, …))`.
      - *Uniqueness*: two functions that satisfy the clauses and agree on atoms and congruence items agree on every item, by the same induction.
      - *Consequences*: `v(~A) = v(A → ⊥) = 1 − v(A)` because `v(⊥) = 0`; `v(A ∨ ~A) = max(v(A), 1 − v(A)) = 1`; vacuous explosion follows from (C4).
      - *Cover*: `B ≠ ⊥_B` and `k ≥ 1`. If `B ⊩⁺ A`, downward heredity (§3 item 9) gives `B*ᵢ ⊩⁺ A` on every cell, so `vᵢ(A) = 1` by (C4), which holds on every boundary. Conversely, if `vᵢ(A) = 1` for every `i`, every cell holds a `Live` `+A`, and Standalone Cover-Gluing (§3 item 12), which is stated for either polarity (`B_i ⊩± E` uniformly), gives `B ⊩⁺ A`. The same two steps with `-` give the negative case. If the cell values are mixed, heredity rules out both global polarities, so `A` is `split`. A classical tautology `T` has `vᵢ(T) = 1` on every cell, since each `vᵢ` is a classical valuation by part 1, so `B ⊩⁺ T`. ∎
