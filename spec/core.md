# Unbound Logic Core Specification (`UL-Core`)

This document is the normative formal specification for the core system (`UL-Core`) and its explicit boundary specializations (`UL-Minimal`, `UL-Intuitionistic`, `UL-Classical`). It formalizes the Phase 1 consensus recorded in [docs/design-review.md](../docs/design-review.md) and obeys the semantic boundaries in [docs/glossary.md](../docs/glossary.md) and [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

Every normative item below carries one label from the claim status ladder of [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) §2 item 5, checked by `spec-guard claims` and `spec-graph`. An item held at `Decided` is ratified, but the reference kernel does not yet meet it; its audit status names the ratified fix in [docs/design-review.md](../docs/design-review.md) §7.

## 1. Syntax, Stratification & Content Grammar

1. `Tested` **Role-Indexed Atomic Items (`G3`)** ([`layer5-content-and-coreference`](../docs/design-review.md#layer5-content-and-coreference)):
   - A signature specifies relation symbols `R` with finite role sets `{r₁, ..., r_n}` (`n ≥ 0`), handle variables `x, y, ...`, handle constants/terms `t, u, w, ...`, and boundary expressions `B, D, ...`.
   - An atomic evaluable item has the role-indexed relational form `R(r₁: t₁, ..., r_n: t_n)`. Unary predicates `P(t)` are the `n = 1` special case; processes, measurements, and multi-party relations are first-class without substance-attribute priority.
   - Reserved atomic forms:
     - **Unsatisfiable item `⊥`**: an item that admits no witness on any non-empty boundary (`B ≠ ⊥_B`) and is structurally excluded on every non-empty boundary.
     - **Boundary-scoped equivalence `t₁ ≈_B t₂`**: asserts congruence of handles `t₁` and `t₂` with respect to all relations native to boundary `B`.

2. `Tested` **Compound Evaluable Items**:
   - Evaluable items `A, C` at stratification level `n ∈ ℕ` are inductively generated by:
     ```text
     A, C ::= R(r₁: t₁, ..., r_n: t_n)
            | ⊥
            | t₁ ≈_B t₂
            | ~A                     (strong polarity flip)
            | A ∧ C                  (conjunction)
            | A ∨ C                  (cover-witnessed disjunction)
            | A → C                  (scoped conditional)
            | ∀(x : D) A(x)          (boundary-scoped universal)
            | ∃(x : D) A(x)          (boundary-scoped existential)
            | Refl_k(J)              (reflection on a judgment J of strictly lower level k < n)
     ```
   - **Structural Impossibility Shorthand**: `¬_⊥ A` is defined as `A → ⊥`.

   - Every item `A` and judgment carries a stratification level `n ∈ ℕ` (and, during vertical evolution, an inquiry stage index `t ∈ ℕ`).

## 2. Boundary Algebra & Inquiry State Dynamics

4. `Tested` **Boundary Meet-Semilattice `(B, ⊑, ⊓, ⊤_B, ⊥_B)`** ([`layer1-scope-factorization`](../docs/design-review.md#layer1-scope-factorization), [`layer3-boundary-composition`](../docs/design-review.md#layer3-boundary-composition)):
   - A **Domain Boundary `B`** is a functional constraint-frame role (a condition held fixed as `B` in one inquiry may be evaluated as an item `A` in another).
   - Boundaries form a bounded meet-semilattice:
     - **Refinement (`⊑`)**: reflexive, antisymmetric (up to boundary equivalence), transitive partial order (`B' ⊑ B` reads "`B'` is a sub-regime at least as restrictive as `B`").
     - **Meet / Overlap (`⊓`)**: idempotent, commutative, associative greatest lower bound (`B₁ ⊓ B₂`). The registry of `Σ` settles a meet only in part, so overlap is three-valued: two boundaries are **known to overlap** when one refines the other, a meet is registered for them, or they have a unique maximal registered common sub-boundary (several call for a registered meet); they are **disjoint** (`B₁ ⊓ B₂ = ⊥_B`) only when one of them is `⊥_B` or disjointness is declared for them or inherited from declared ancestors; otherwise their overlap is **unknown**. An unknown overlap is not disjointness: it never satisfies a disjointness premise, licenses no Scoped Cut, and opposite records across it carry an `OverlapUnknown` obligation (item 14; [`audit-unregistered-meet-closed-world`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-unregistered-meet-closed-world)). A registered meet is the greatest lower bound of its pair: every boundary that refines both members refines the meet, an inferred refinement held to domain monotonicity (item 6) and to declared disjointness. The inference is applied until nothing changes and counts wherever refinement does: registering a meet therefore also settles an ambiguity among several maximal common sub-boundaries; no non-empty boundary may lie below two disjoint ones, so a later disjointness declaration or boundary definition that would break this is refused, as for declared refinements; and a meet is refused when its inferred refinements would break domain monotonicity, put a boundary below two disjoint ones, or make two distinct boundaries refine each other.
     - **Top (`⊤_B`)**: unrestricted global frame (`B ⊑ ⊤_B` for all `B`).
     - **Empty / Impossible Boundary (`⊥_B`)**: bottom element (`⊥_B ⊑ B` for all `B`). **Non-Witnessing Law**: `⊥_B` has no realizable domain and **cannot supply any genuine positive Witness (`+`)**.
   - **Adjoining a Constraint (`B ⊓ [A]`)**: Where an evaluable item `A` is promoted to a boundary constraint `[A]`, structural collapse occurs iff `B ⊓ [A] = ⊥_B`.

5. `Tested` **Witnessed Exhaustive Covers (`{B_i} ▷ B`)** ([`layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers`](../docs/design-review.md#layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers)):
   - A finite family `{B₁, ..., B_k}` is a **witnessed exhaustive cover** of `B` (written `{B_i} ▷ B` or `B = ⨆_{i=1}^k B_i`) only when structurally witnessed under the following finitary coverage axioms:
     1. **Refinement**: For every `i`, `B_i ⊑ B`.
     2. **Identity**: `{B} ▷ B` for every `B ≠ ⊥_B`; `∅ ▷ ⊥_B`.
     3. **Meet-Stability (Base Change)**: If `{B_i} ▷ B`, `B' ⊑ B`, and each `B_i` is known to overlap `B'` or disjoint from it (item 4), then `{B_i ⊓ B' | B_i known to overlap B'} ▷ B'`. A cell whose overlap with `B'` is unknown may hold part of `B'`, so the cover has no base change to `B'` until that overlap is settled.
     4. **Transitivity (Composition)**: If `{B_i} ▷ B` and `{B_{i,j}} ▷ B_i` for every `i`, then `{B_{i,j}} ▷ B`.
     5. **Non-Vacuity**: For every `B ≠ ⊥_B`, `∅ ▷ B` never holds.
   - **Validity as the boundary system grows** ([`audit-cover-non-vacuity-drift`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-cover-non-vacuity-drift)): a cover registered by `WitnessCover` (item 7) is judged against the current boundaries of `Σ`, not only when it is registered, so the order in which boundaries and covers are registered does not change the outcome. It is **invalid** when some non-empty `B' ⊑ B` is disjoint from every cell, which Axioms 3 and 5 together forbid; **unverified** when it is not invalid but some non-empty `B' ⊑ B` is known to overlap no cell and has an unknown overlap with at least one (item 4); and **valid** otherwise. An invalid or unverified cover witnesses nothing: it supports no cover-witnessed disjunction, `∨E` or Cover-Gluing (items 12 and 14), and no cover is derived from it; an unverified cover keeps its obligation until its unsettled sub-boundaries are settled ([`audit-unverified-cover-authority`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-unverified-cover-authority)). Each invalid or unverified cover carries a revision obligation that names it and those sub-boundaries `B'`; settling their overlaps or withdrawing the cover (`Withdraw`, item 7) discharges it. The sub-boundaries `B'` above range over the boundaries of `Σ` below `B`, `B` included, and a `⊥_B` cell is disjoint from every boundary and is left out of the witnessed cover. The obligation of an invalid cover names the sub-boundaries that refute it, and that of an unverified cover those that leave it unsettled; a valid cover whose base change to some `B'` is blocked by a cell of unknown overlap owes nothing. The **witnessed covers** of `Σ` are the valid registered covers, closed under Axioms 2–4, where a derived cover is judged by the same rule and is not derived when it is invalid. The registry admits no non-empty common sub-boundary of two disjoint boundaries and keeps every registered meet the greatest lower bound of its pair (item 4), so covers derived from valid registered covers are valid: every witnessed cover is valid, and no base change is empty.

6. `Decided` **Co-Reference & Individuation Across Updates (`≈_B`, `I_B`)** ([`layer5-content-and-coreference`](../docs/design-review.md#layer5-content-and-coreference)):
   - **Audit status**: the many-sorted reading of `Observe±` ratified in [`audit-free-handle-policy`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-free-handle-policy) is not yet specified or enforced.
   - Each handle `t` admissible in `B` (written `t ∈ Dom(B)`) carries an explicit boundary-relative **individuation criterion `I_B(t)`**.
   - **Domain Monotonicity**: whenever `B' ⊑ B`, `Dom(B') ⊆ Dom(B)`; a sub-regime never admits individuals its parents lack, so a boundary with several parents admits only handles common to all of them, and one below both members of a pair with a registered meet only handles of the meet (item 4). The eigenvariable typing of `∀E` (`eigenDom ⊑ D`) relies on this property, ratified with the domain closure of [`audit-universal-existential-import`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-universal-existential-import).
   - `≈_B` is a witnessed congruence on `Dom(B)`: reflexive, symmetric, transitive on unquarantined grounds, and substitutive for every relation `R` native to `B`:
     - If `B ⊩⁺ (t₁ ≈_B t₂)` and `B ⊩⁺ R(..., r: t₁, ...)`, then `B ⊩⁺ R(..., r: t₂, ...)` (and identically for `⊩⁻`), recording provenance on both premises.
   - **Multi-Granularity Isolation**: Refining `B_macro` to a finer regime `B_micro ⊑ B_macro` may introduce finer relations not native to `B_macro`; `t₁ ≈_{B_macro} t₂` and `t₁ ≉_{B_micro} t₂` coexist without conflict because congruence is indexed by its boundary.
   - **Anti-Equivocation**: In a vertical update `Σ_t → Σ_{t+1}`, new evidence concerning handle `t'` interacts with prior grounds on `(B, A(t))` only if `B ⊩⁺ (t' ≈_B t)` is witnessed under `I_B`.

7. `Decided` **Inquiry States `Σ`, Vertical Updates & Anti-Immunization (`M1`, `M2`)** ([`zetetic-friction-source`](../docs/design-review.md#zetetic-friction-source), [`layer3-boundary-composition`](../docs/design-review.md#layer3-boundary-composition), [`layer5-content-and-coreference`](../docs/design-review.md#layer5-content-and-coreference)):
   - **Audit status**: M1(i) below still uses the undirected graph that [`audit-m1-indirect-overblocking`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-m1-indirect-overblocking) replaces, and boundary declarations are not yet transitions ([`audit-boundary-mutation-staging`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-boundary-mutation-staging)); the ratified resolutions are not yet specified here.
   - An **Inquiry State `Σ`** is a finite state tuple `(G⁺, G⁻, C_B, O_rev)` containing base positive witnesses `G⁺`, base negative exclusions `G⁻`, declared boundary constraints/covers/incompatibilities `C_B`, each constraint carrying an identifier, and open **Revision Obligations `O_rev`**.
   - Vertical evolution `Σ₀ → Σ₁ → ...` is **ordered and non-commutative** (`Exchange` does not hold vertically) and proceeds via six explicit transitions. Base observations, recorded theorems and constraints carry identifiers, each naming one transition for the life of the inquiry: `Retract` and `Withdraw` do not free them for reuse.
     1. **`Observe⁺(B, A, w)` / `Observe⁻(B, A, w)`**: Add a base witness or exclusion anchored by primitive justification `w` on non-empty `B ≠ ⊥_B`.
     2. **`Retract(g)` (Pure Cancellation, Sanskrit *prasajya-pratiṣedha*)** ([`layer4-negation-vs-exclusion`](../docs/design-review.md#layer4-negation-vs-exclusion)): Withdraw a specific base ground `g` (e.g., discredited observation or defective refutation) from `G⁺` or `G⁻`, recomputing derived grounds via provenance DAGs so `(B, A)` returns toward `Open` without fabricating the opposite polarity.
     3. **`RefineBoundary(B → B' = B ⊓ [δ])`**: Discharge a collision on `B` caused by counterexample `-A(w)` by narrowing the regime to `B'`, which excises at least one handle of `Dom(B)`, for a target relation that occurs in `A`, strictly subject to BOTH anti-immunization laws:
        - **M2 (Non-Erasure Scope-Shrinkage)**: Narrowing `B` to `B'` never erases the refutation on `B`; `(B, ∀(x : B) A(x))` remains `Excluded (-)` while `(B', ∀(x : B') A(x))` carries explicit narrowing provenance.
        - **M1 (Structural Admissibility)**: The splitting condition `δ(x)` must satisfy (i) **Non-Circularity** (`δ` does not reference the target relation of `A`, directly or through the relation-dependency graph that links the relations occurring together in a registered incompatibility or structural collapse of `C_B` or in a conditional that is `Live` on some boundary), (ii) **Non-Contingent Structural Definability** (`δ` cannot exclude `w` merely by its contingent empirical handle tag `x ≉ specimen_402`, while permitting structurally defined singularities of `B`'s own operations such as the additive identity `0` in `ℝ \ {0}`), and (iii) **Independent Excess-Content Witness (Anti-Proxy Guard)** (whenever contingent empirical handles are excised into a non-empty sub-boundary `B'`, `δ(u)` must carry a **`Live` positive witness (`+δ(u)`)** on at least one non-refuted surviving handle `u ∈ Dom(B')` distinct from the excised contingent handles, and **no** surviving handle `v ∈ Dom(B')` may witness `Live -δ(v)`), and (iv) **Domain Binding** (`Dom(B') = {h ∈ Dom(B) : ¬Live -δ(h)}`, and every handle excised from `Dom(B)` carries `Live -δ(h)` on `B`; a handle whose `δ` is `Open` stays in `B'`, and the admitted refinement stays recorded in `Σ`, so a later `Live -δ(v)` on a handle `v` kept in `B'` records a revision obligation against it).
     4. **`Constrain(A ⊥_B C)` / `WitnessCover({B_i} ▷ B)`**: Register a witnessed material incompatibility or verified exhaustive cover in `C_B`.
     5. **`CloseDomain(D)`**: Register in `C_B`, for a boundary `D ≠ ⊥_B`, a witnessed claim that the handles of `Dom(D)` exhaust `D`. The closure anchors the handle set it witnessed and becomes invalid if `Dom(D)` changes; it gates the enumerative grounding of `+∀` and `-∃` over `D` (item 12, item 14) and does not pass to sub-boundaries of `D` ([`audit-universal-existential-import`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-universal-existential-import)).
     6. **`Withdraw(c)` (Pure Cancellation in `C_B`)** ([`audit-cover-non-vacuity-drift`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-cover-non-vacuity-drift)): Remove the constraint `c` of `C_B` (an incompatibility, structural collapse, cover or closure), named by its identifier, without adding anything to `Σ`, so a faulty constraint is revised at its source instead of by retracting correct observations. Derived grounds are recomputed as after `Retract`, and the items `c` named stay tracked (item 14).

## 3. Dual-Polarity Semantics, Provenance & Derivation Calculus

8. `Tested` **Dual-Polarity Judgments & Well-Founded DAG Provenance** ([`layer1-dual-polarity`](../docs/design-review.md#layer1-dual-polarity), [`layer3-structural-rules-and-cut`](../docs/design-review.md#layer3-structural-rules-and-cut)):
   - For non-empty `B ≠ ⊥_B` in state `Σ`, the two primitive judgment forms are:
     - `Σ ; B ⊩⁺ A [π]` — `A` has positive **Witness** on `B` justified by provenance DAG `π`.
     - `Σ ; B ⊩⁻ A [π]` — `A` has negative **Exclusion** on `B` justified by provenance DAG `π`.
   - **Well-Foundedness Law**: Every valid provenance `π` is a finite **acyclic DAG** whose leaves are base grounds in `G⁺ ∪ G⁻` or structural axioms in `C_B`. Mutual-support cycles confer zero grounding.
   - **Derived Local Statuses** of `(B, A)` in `Σ`:
     - `Open`: no valid `π⁺` for `B ⊩⁺ A` and no valid `π⁻` for `B ⊩⁻ A`.
     - `Grounded`: at least one valid `π⁺` and no valid `π⁻`.
     - `Excluded`: at least one valid `π⁻` and no valid `π⁺`.
     - `Collision`: at least one valid `π⁺` and at least one valid `π⁻` on `B`.
   - **Two Axes** ([`audit-status-obstruction-mismatch`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-status-obstruction-mismatch)): a local status reads the ground records on `(B, A)` alone.     - **Definition**: `Collision`, obstruction and `Live` are defined by the evaluation program of item 14; this item describes their consequences. 12. `Tested` **Connective & Quantifier Polarity Rules (on non-empty `B ≠ ⊥_B`)** ([`layer4-negation-vs-exclusion`](../docs/design-review.md#layer4-negation-vs-exclusion), [`layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier`](../docs/design-review.md#layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier), [`layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers`](../docs/design-review.md#layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers), [`layer5-content-and-coreference`](../docs/design-review.md#layer5-content-and-coreference)):
    - **Unsatisfiable Item `⊥`**:
      - `⊩⁺ ⊥`: base observations obey `⊥ ∉ G⁺` on every non-empty `B ≠ ⊥_B`, and no non-empty `B ≠ ⊥_B` ever holds a `Live` positive witness `B ⊩⁺ ⊥`.
      - `⊩⁻ ⊥`: holds structurally on every `B ≠ ⊥_B`.
    - **Strong Polarity Flip (`~A`, Sanskrit *paryudāsa*)**:
      - `B ⊩⁺ (~A)` ⟺ `B ⊩⁻ A`
      - `B ⊩⁻ (~A)` ⟺ `B ⊩⁺ A`
    - **Material Incompatibility (`A ⊥_B C ∈ C_B`)**:
      - Inherits downward across every non-empty sub-boundary `B' ⊑ B` (`B' ≠ ⊥_B`): if `B' ⊩⁺ A [π]` is `Live`, derive `B' ⊩⁻ C [Incompat(π, A ⊥_B C)]`; symmetrically, `Live` `B' ⊩⁺ C [π]` derives `B' ⊩⁻ A [Incompat(π, A ⊥_B C)]`.
    - **Structural Impossibility (`A → ⊥`)**:
      - If adjoining `A` as a boundary constraint collapses `B` (`B ⊓ [A] = ⊥_B`), derive `B ⊩⁺ (A → ⊥)` and `B ⊩⁻ A`; moreover, any `Live` `B ⊩⁺ (A → ⊥)` induces `B ⊩⁻ A` (`imposs-excl`), while double structural impossibility `(A → ⊥) → ⊥` derives only `-(A → ⊥)` and does **not** derive `B ⊩⁺ A`.
    - **Conjunction (`A ∧ C`)**:
      - `B ⊩⁺ (A ∧ C)` ⟺ `B ⊩⁺ A` and `B ⊩⁺ C` (both `Live`, joining DAGs).
      - `B ⊩⁻ (A ∧ C)` ⟺ `B ⊩⁻ A` or `B ⊩⁻ C`.
    - **Cover-Witnessed Disjunction (`A ∨ C`) & Standalone Cover-Gluing**:
      - `B ⊩⁺ (A ∨ C)` ⟺ there exists a witnessed finite exhaustive cover `{B₁, ..., B_k} ▷ B` (item 5) such that for each non-empty cell `B_i`, `B_i ⊩⁺ A` (`Live`) or `B_i ⊩⁺ C` (`Live`).
      - `B ⊩⁻ (A ∨ C)` ⟺ `B ⊩⁻ A` and `B ⊩⁻ C`.
      - **Cover Gluing (`∨E`)**: If `B ⊩⁺ (A ∨ C)` via `{B_i} ▷ B`, `B ⊩⁺ (A → E)` (`Live`), and `B ⊩⁺ (C → E)` (`Live`), then `B ⊩⁺ E` (grounded by gluing the per-cell derivations across `{B_i}`).
      - **Standalone Cover-Gluing (`Sheaf Gluing`)**: If `{B_i} ▷ B` (item 5) and every non-empty cell satisfies `B_i ⊩± E` (`Live`) uniformly for a polarity `±`, then `B ⊩± E` is grounded by gluing across `{B_i}`.
    - **Scoped Conditional (`A → C`)**:
      - `B ⊩⁺ (A → C)`: witnessed either by a verified transmission rule mapping any `Live` `B' ⊩⁺ A [π]` on any non-empty `B' ⊑ B` to `B' ⊩⁺ C [f(π)]`, or by a verified **`→I` hypothetical sub-proof box** deriving `(B ⊩⁺ C)` from a lexically scoped local assumption `A` without importing un-`Live` grounds or exploding from `-A` (the local assumption `A` does not collide with an ambient `B ⊩⁻ A`, preserving `A → A` and constructive weakening `C ⊢ A → C`, while imported `Live` grounds preserve their base-leaf and intermediate-ground provenance). Mere absence of `+A`, or defeasible `-A`, does **not** fabricate `+(A → C)` in `UL-Core`.
      - `Live B ⊩⁺ A` and `B ⊩⁻ C` ⇒ `B ⊩⁻ (A → C)` (one-way counterexample introduction gated on `Live +A`, so directly collided `+A` cannot refute `A → C`; preserves symmetric downward heredity without leaking counterexamples across disjoint sibling boundaries; `B ⊩⁻ (A → ⊥)` derived via `imposs-excl` does **not** invert into `B ⊩⁺ A`, preventing Glivenko double impossibility from collapsing into DNE or LEM; any counterexample on `B' ⊑ B` yields `B' ⊩⁻ (A → C)`, colliding with inherited `B' ⊩⁺ (A → C)` and triggering upstream obstruction of `(B, +(A → C))` per item 10).
    - **Boundary-Scoped Quantifiers (`∀(x : D) A(x)`, `∃(x : D) A(x)`, with optional constraint `D ⊓ [δ]`, evaluated at `B`)**:
      - `B ⊩⁺ ∀(x : D) A(x)` ⟺ generic structural grounding showing `B ⊩⁺ A(α)` either by enumeration over `Dom(D)` when `CloseDomain(D)` is witnessed and `D ≠ ⊥_B` (every handle carries `Live -δ(x)` when `[δ]` is present, or else `Live +A(x)` together with `Live +δ(x)` when `[δ]` is present; an empty `D_δ` or an empty `Dom(D)` yields vacuous truth grounded by those exclusions and the closure) , by Cover-Gluing over a witnessed cover of the evaluation boundary `B` (item 14), or via syntactic **schematic `∀I`** deriving `(B ⊩⁺ A(α))` for an **eigenvariable `α`** strictly fresh with respect to `Σ` (every handle of an item it tracks, retracted observations and withdrawn constraints included, item 14), active outer assumptions, registered domain handles, `domainConstraint`, and the quantifier template (`∀I` discharges a local assumption `+δ(α)`; `∀E` instantiates `Live +∀` across any active scoped eigenvariable or admissible term, requiring a verified `+δ(t)` proof whenever `[δ]` is present).

13. `Tested` **Four-Tier Deductive Spectrum** ([`scope-anchor-unified-core`](../docs/design-review.md#scope-anchor-unified-core), [`layer2-classical-boundary-recovery`](../docs/design-review.md#layer2-classical-boundary-recovery)):
    - Every specialization below is obtained purely by imposing explicit closure constraints on `(B, Σ)` without altering or contradicting items 1–12 of `UL-Core`:
    1. **`UL-Core`** (General Regime): Items 1–12 with no additional closure constraints. Supports partial information (`Open`), localized `Collision` with Quarantine, non-monotonic vertical revision, multi-granularity `≈_B`, and stratified reflection.
    2. **`UL-Minimal`** (Minimal Constructive Specialization):
       - *Closure conditions*: Restrict vertical transitions to monotone growth (forbid `Retract` and `Withdraw`) and enforce **`No-Gluts`** (no `(B, A)` is in `Collision`).
       - *Consequences*: Quarantine never triggers; every valid ground is `Live`; `Scoped Cut` becomes unrestricted `Cut`. The proof checker's rules for `∧`, `∨` (cover-witnessed), `→`, `∀`, `∃`, `A → ⊥` and `~A` are those of minimal natural deduction `NJ_min`, with Nelson-style strong negation `~A` alongside `A → ⊥`, and the checker rejects derivations of Excluded Middle and of Double Negation Elimination for `A → ⊥` ([metatheory.md](metatheory.md) MT-8a).
    3. **`UL-Intuitionistic`** (Heyting Constructive Specialization):
       - *Closure conditions*: `UL-Minimal` plus the boundary axiom **`⊥ → C`** for every item `C` (sound under `No-Gluts` because `⊥` has no witness on any non-empty `B ≠ ⊥_B`, so Modus Ponens never fires from `⊥`).

The items considered mention only handles of `Dom(B*)`, and every quantifier domain `D` they use satisfies `Dom(D) ⊆ Dom(B*)`, is finite and non-empty, and has a witnessed closure `CloseDomain(D)`.

## 5. Evaluation Program

14. `Decided` **Evaluation Program `Π(Σ)` and Well-Founded Authority** ([`audit-spec-kernel-coupling`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-spec-kernel-coupling), [`audit-universal-existential-import`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-universal-existential-import)):
    - **Audit status**: the `Theorem` row does not yet track the constraints a theorem uses; the ratified resolution of [`audit-theorem-dependency-gaps`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-theorem-dependency-gaps) is not yet specified here.
    - **Role**: `Π(Σ)` fixes, for an inquiry state `Σ` and a finite set `Q` of queried items, which ground records exist on which boundaries, which of them collide, which are obstructed, and which are transmission-authorized. Its meaning is the well-founded model of the program (Van Gelder, Ross and Schlipf, 1991). Items 8–12 describe consequences of this item; where a description there disagrees with this item, this item governs. An implementation is a projection of `Π(Σ)`: where it cannot reach the fixpoint within its bounds, or where a meet the program needs is ambiguous, it reports an error instead of returning a truncated or guessed result. A meet is needed by a Scoped Cut instance over the pair when its `Live` premises are true in the least model of the program read with every `obs` atom false and with the Scoped Cut instances over ambiguous pairs left out, since otherwise the instance can never fire; by the reporting of `OverlapUnknown` for a pair holding opposite present records; by the judgment of a registered cover for a sub-boundary known to overlap no cell, when no sub-boundary refutes the cover (an ambiguous pair refutes no cover, so judging a derived cover never needs one); and by a base change that no cell of unknown overlap blocks. Elsewhere an ambiguous pair is not an error. This item is complete on its own: the clean-room oracle's notes on how it read the item (`test/oracle/AMBIGUITIES.md`) are a non-normative record, and a reading becomes part of the specification only when it is written here.
    - **Universe**:
      - `𝔅(Σ)` is the set of non-empty boundaries declared in `Σ`, including `⊤_B`; every quantification over sub-boundaries in this item ranges over `𝔅(Σ)`, and `⊑` is reflexive.
      - The items **tracked** by `Σ` are `⊥` and every item named by a transition in the history of `Σ`, those of retracted observations and withdrawn constraints included: the items of base observations and recorded theorems, both sides of each incompatibility `A ⊥_B C`, both `A` and `A → ⊥` for each structural collapse `B ⊓ [A] = ⊥_B`, and the universal of each `RefineBoundary` transition with the criterion instances `δ(h)` for every handle `h` of its original boundary. A retracted observation stops supplying a record, and a withdrawn constraint stops applying, but their items stay in the universe, so the non-erasure of M2 (item 7) stays observable.
      - `U(Σ, Q)` is the set of tracked items and the items of `Q`, closed under direct sub-items, under quantifier instances `A(w)` and `δ(w)` for `w ∈ Dom(D)` (a quantifier contributes its instances, not its open body), and under congruence rewrites: replacing the handle at one position of a relational atom or of an `≈` item by a handle of a boundary in `𝔅(Σ)`. A polarity flip belongs to `U(Σ, Q)` exactly when it arises in this way; `U(Σ, Q)` is not closed under prefixing `~` to its members. `U(Σ, Q)` is finite because `Σ`, `Q`, the boundaries and their domains are finite.
    - **Atoms**: for `B ∈ 𝔅(Σ)`, `A ∈ U(Σ, Q)` and `p ∈ {+, -}`: `rec(B, p, A)`, a ground record of polarity `p` for `A` on `B`; `live(B, p, A)`, that record is transmission-authorized; `coll(B, A)`; and `obs(B, A)`. A rule instance whose conclusion lies outside `U(Σ, Q)` does not exist, and a premise outside `U(Σ, Q)` never holds; in particular, `∨E` ranges over the items `E` with both `A → E` and `C → E` in `U(Σ, Q)`.
    - **Program**: negation occurs only through `obs`.
      ```text
      coll(B, A) ← rec(B, +, A), rec(B, -, A)
      obs(B, A)  ← coll(B', A)                          for every B' ∈ 𝔅(Σ) with B' ⊑ B

      for every instance of a rule below with conclusion (B, p, A),
      record premises R and Live premises L:
        rec(B, p, A)  ← rec(P) for each P ∈ R, live(P) for each P ∈ L
        live(B, p, A) ← live(P) for each P ∈ R ∪ L, not obs(B, A)
      ```
    - **Firing principle**: positive conclusions, and exclusions inferred from positive evidence, take `Live` premises. The exclusion of a compound takes the exclusion records of its components as record premises, while its context premises (the antecedent of a counterexample, domain membership `±δ`, cover cells) are `Live`. A record from record premises exists so that conflicts surface and obstruct upward; it transmits only when all its premises are `Live`.

    - **Reporting**, in the well-founded model `M` of `Π(Σ)`:
      - A record of `(B, p, A)` is **present** when `rec(B, p, A)` is true in `M`; a record whose atom is undefined is possible but not present, and no status rests on it.
      - `(B, A)` is in `Collision` when `coll(B, A)` is true or undefined in `M`, so a possible conflict is never hidden, and each such `(B, A)` carries a revision obligation `Rev(B, A)`. For two boundaries whose overlap is unknown (item 4), each item `A` with a present `+A` record on one and a present `-A` record on the other carries an obligation `OverlapUnknown(B₁, B₂, A)`; no `Collision` is reported for it, since no boundary holds both records. There is one such obligation for each unordered pair of distinct boundaries and item, whichever of the two holds `+A`; present records raise it whatever their authority, inherited records included, also when either slot is itself in `Collision`, and it is not a `Rev(B, A)`. Otherwise its local status is `Grounded`, `Excluded` or `Open` according to which of its records are present.
      - The authority of a present record is `Live` when `live(B, p, A)` is true in `M`, `Undetermined` when it is undefined, and `Quarantined` when it is false; an absent record has no authority. Only `Live` records transmit, so a symmetric conflict reads `Collision` with `Undetermined` authority on both sides and transmits on neither.
      - A provenance DAG of a `Live` record is any finite derivation of it by rule instances whose premises are `Live`. It explains the record; it does not define it.
      - **Query conservativity**: when every handle named by the items of `Σ` and of `Q` belongs to a domain of `𝔅(Σ)`, adding items to `Q` changes neither the local status nor the authority of any slot over `U(Σ, ∅)`, nor `No-Gluts`. A handle outside every domain can break this, since congruence rewrites reach only handles of `𝔅(Σ)` ([`audit-free-handle-policy`](../docs/design-review.md#audit-free-handle-policy)).
    - **Constraints**: "`{Bᵢ} ▷ B` witnessed" means a witnessed cover of item 5, so an invalid cover supplies no rule instance; "in `C_B`" means registered by a transition of item 7 and not withdrawn.
