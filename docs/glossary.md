# Terminology Registry

This registry enforces Guardrail 3 (**Terminology Hygiene**) from [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). Every loaded term inherited from classical or modern formal logic is explicitly quarantined until its local semantic boundary is settled; every local primitive or derived term links to the governing decision in [docs/design-review.md](design-review.md).

## 1. Quarantined Classical Terms

Do **not** use these terms as unexamined primitives in specification prose or formal rules. When referencing them for historical comparison, mark the classical context explicitly.

| Term | Classical / Inherited Meaning | Local Operational Boundary | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Truth** | Static, context-free bivalent property (`T`) often presupposing mind-independent correspondence or Platonic abstracta. | Suspended as a primitive. Replaced at the ground floor by scoped **Witness (`+`)** and **Exclusion (`-`)** under [`layer1-dual-polarity`](design-review.md#layer1-dual-polarity); classical truth-aptness is targeted only as a boundary specialization ([`scope-anchor-unified-core`](design-review.md#scope-anchor-unified-core)). | quarantined |
| **Proposition** | Context-free, timeless declarative sentence determinately carrying `T` or `F`. | Suspended as the primary unit. Replaced by **Role-Indexed Relational Claims** (`R(r₁: t₁, ..., r_n: t_n)`) indexed by **Boundary (`B`)** and **Inquiry State (`Σ`)** ([`layer1-scope-factorization`](design-review.md#layer1-scope-factorization), [`layer5-content-and-coreference`](design-review.md#layer5-content-and-coreference)). | quarantined |
| **Negation (`¬`)** | Single univocal Boolean complement (`¬¬A ≡ A`) conflating active refutation, withdrawal of support, and logical impossibility. | Disentangled into **Strong Polarity Flip (`~A`)**, **Pure Cancellation (`Retract` in `Σ`)**, and **Structural Impossibility (`A → ⊥`)**, unified with **Material Incompatibility (`A ⊥_B C`)** ([`layer4-negation-vs-exclusion`](design-review.md#layer4-negation-vs-exclusion)). | quarantined |
| **Material Implication** | Classical truth-functional shortcut (`¬A ∨ C`) under which any false antecedent vacuously implies any conclusion. | Suspended in the general core. Replaced by **Scoped Conditional (`A → C`)** requiring verified witness transmission across sub-boundaries ([`layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier`](design-review.md#layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier)); material implication is targeted only as a `No-Gaps` + `No-Gluts` boundary specialization. | quarantined |
| **Consistency** | Syntactic/semantic absence of deriving `A ∧ ¬A` (tied to classical Explosion). | Replaced locally by **Collision-Free** (no overlapping `(B, A)` holds both `+` and `-` in `Σ`) and **Non-Empty Boundary (`B ≠ ⊥_B`)** ([`layer1-collision-quarantine`](design-review.md#layer1-collision-quarantine), [`layer3-boundary-composition`](design-review.md#layer3-boundary-composition), [`layer4-negation-vs-exclusion`](design-review.md#layer4-negation-vs-exclusion)). | quarantined |
| **Contradiction** | Conjunction `A ∧ ¬A` triggering global Explosion (`Ex Falso Quodlibet`). | Distinguished from **Collision** (co-presence of `+` and `-` on overlapping `B` in `Σ`), which triggers local **Inference Quarantine** and **Revision Obligation** rather than Explosion ([`layer1-collision-quarantine`](design-review.md#layer1-collision-quarantine)). | quarantined |
| **Completeness** | Semantic completeness (`⊨` implies `⊢`) or syntactic completeness (every sentence or its negation is provable, subject to Gödelian limits). | Never use informally to mean "finished." Use **end-to-end** for architectural coverage and **No-Gaps** for epistemic/boundary closure where every item is either `+` or `-`. | quarantined |
| **Validity / Proof** | Classical truth-preservation (`⊨`) or monotone finitary derivation (`⊢`) over static premises. | Replaced in the general core by **Scoped Witness Transmission** backed by **Well-Founded Provenance (DAG)** and **Scoped Cut** ([`layer3-structural-rules-and-cut`](design-review.md#layer3-structural-rules-and-cut), [`layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier`](design-review.md#layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier)); classical `⊨` / `⊢` remain targeted only under boundary recovery ([`layer2-classical-boundary-recovery`](design-review.md#layer2-classical-boundary-recovery)). | quarantined |
| **Identity / Object** | Discrete enduring individual (`x`) governed by absolute Leibnizian equality (`x = y`) over a fixed quantifier domain. | Suspended as context-free substance/equality primitives. Replaced by **Role-Labeled Handles (`t`)** and **Boundary-Scoped Equivalence (`t₁ ≈_B t₂`)** ([`layer5-content-and-coreference`](design-review.md#layer5-content-and-coreference)); classical Leibnizian `x = y` is recovered only on finest closed atomic `B*` ([`layer2-classical-boundary-recovery`](design-review.md#layer2-classical-boundary-recovery)). | quarantined |

## 2. Working Primitives & Local Terms

| Term | Operational Definition | Governing Entry | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Witness / Grounding (`+`)** | Irreducible positive polarity: an explicit basis, construction, or evidence supporting an evaluable item `A` under boundary `B` in inquiry state `Σ`. | [`layer1-dual-polarity`](design-review.md#layer1-dual-polarity) | decided |
| **Exclusion / Refutation (`-`)** | Irreducible negative polarity: an explicit obstruction, incompatibility, or counterexample ruling out `A` under boundary `B` in inquiry state `Σ`. | [`layer1-dual-polarity`](design-review.md#layer1-dual-polarity) | decided |
| **Domain Boundary (`B`)** | Functional constraint-frame role delimiting the regime and applicability conditions of a claim, structured as a meet-semilattice (`⊑`, `⊓`, `⊥_B`). | [`layer1-scope-factorization`](design-review.md#layer1-scope-factorization), [`layer3-boundary-composition`](design-review.md#layer3-boundary-composition) | decided |
| **Boundary Refinement (`B' ⊑ B`) & Meet (`B₁ ⊓ B₂`)** | `B' ⊑ B` marks `B'` as a sub-regime at least as restrictive as `B`; `B₁ ⊓ B₂` is the joint overlap where conflicting polarities must collide whenever `B₁ ⊓ B₂ ≠ ⊥_B`. | [`layer3-boundary-composition`](design-review.md#layer3-boundary-composition) | decided |
| **Empty / Impossible Boundary (`⊥_B`)** | Unrealizable boundary regime resulting from incompatible constraints; explicitly prohibited from supplying any genuine positive Witness (`+`). | [`layer3-boundary-composition`](design-review.md#layer3-boundary-composition) | decided |
| **Non-Erasure Scope-Shrinkage (M2)** | Structural anti-immunization rule: narrowing `B` to `B'` to re-ground `A` after a counterexample must preserve `(B, A)` as `Excluded (-)` rather than erasing the broader refutation (distinct from vertical `Retract` of a discredited counterexample). | [`layer3-boundary-composition`](design-review.md#layer3-boundary-composition) | decided |
| **Inquiry State (`Σ`)** | Situated state of accumulated witnesses, exclusions, provenance DAGs, and unresolved obligations that evolves non-monotonically via ordered updates (`Σ₀ → Σ₁`). | [`layer1-scope-factorization`](design-review.md#layer1-scope-factorization), [`layer3-structural-rules-and-cut`](design-review.md#layer3-structural-rules-and-cut) | decided |
| **Open** | Derived status of `(B, A)` in `Σ`: neither witnessed (`+`) nor excluded (`-`). | [`layer1-dual-polarity`](design-review.md#layer1-dual-polarity) | decided |
| **Grounded** | Derived status of `(B, A)` in `Σ`: witnessed (`+`) and not excluded (`-`). | [`layer1-dual-polarity`](design-review.md#layer1-dual-polarity) | decided |
| **Excluded** | Derived status of `(B, A)` in `Σ`: excluded (`-`) and not witnessed (`+`). | [`layer1-dual-polarity`](design-review.md#layer1-dual-polarity) | decided |
| **Collision** | Derived status of `(B, A)` in `Σ`: both witnessed (`+`) and excluded (`-`) over overlapping boundary `B`. Triggers Inference Quarantine and Revision Obligation rather than Explosion. | [`layer1-collision-quarantine`](design-review.md#layer1-collision-quarantine) | decided |
| **Inference Quarantine** | Local containment rule under which a collided `(B, A)` (or any super-boundary obstructed by a sub-boundary collision) loses `Live` transmission authority and cannot fire transmissive rules (`MP`, `∨E`, `∧I`, `Cover-Gluing`, `imposs-excl`, `∃I`) while unresolved, enforced with Van Gelder stratification + source-SCC symmetric conflict locking to prevent ghost collisions. | [`layer1-collision-quarantine`](design-review.md#layer1-collision-quarantine) | decided |
| **Revision Obligation** | Normative tension recorded in `Σ` whenever a `Collision` occurs, requiring discharge via boundary narrowing/splitting or defeat/retraction of a conflicting ground. | [`layer1-collision-quarantine`](design-review.md#layer1-collision-quarantine) | decided |
| **Well-Founded Provenance (DAG)** | Acyclic justification dependency graph carried by every derived ground, anchored in base grounds in `Σ`; cycles confer zero grounding and quarantined intermediates suspend only conclusions lacking an independent uncollided path. | [`layer3-structural-rules-and-cut`](design-review.md#layer3-structural-rules-and-cut) | decided |
| **Scoped Cut** | Horizontal transitivity of inference over uncontested chains on compatible boundary meets (`B₁ ⊓ B₂ ≠ ⊥_B`), blocked whenever an intermediate node enters `Collision`. | [`layer3-structural-rules-and-cut`](design-review.md#layer3-structural-rules-and-cut) | decided |
| **Strong Polarity Flip (`~A`)** | Object-level strong refutation (akin to Sanskrit *paryudāsa*): `+(~A) ⟺ -A` and `-(~A) ⟺ +A`, involutive on polarities without forcing Excluded Middle or Explosion. | [`layer4-negation-vs-exclusion`](design-review.md#layer4-negation-vs-exclusion) | decided |
| **Pure Cancellation (`Retract`)** | Inquiry-state update (akin to Sanskrit *prasajya-pratiṣedha*) withdrawing an unsupported ground or discredited refutation so `(B, A)` returns toward `Open` without smuggling in the opposite polarity. | [`layer4-negation-vs-exclusion`](design-review.md#layer4-negation-vs-exclusion) | decided |
| **Structural Impossibility (`A → ⊥`)** | Exclusion derived when assuming `A` under `B` forces boundary collapse (`B ⊓ [A] = ⊥_B`), where `⊥` is the unsatisfiable item having no witness on any non-empty boundary; double structural impossibility does not yield `+A`. | [`layer4-negation-vs-exclusion`](design-review.md#layer4-negation-vs-exclusion) | decided |
| **Material Incompatibility (`A ⊥_B C`)** | Boundary-level mutual-exclusion constraint in `B` whereby `+A` derives `-C` (with provenance) and `+C` derives `-A`. | [`layer4-negation-vs-exclusion`](design-review.md#layer4-negation-vs-exclusion) | decided |
| **Scoped Conditional (`A → C`)** | Directional conditional on `B`: witnessed (`+`) by verified witness transmission across non-empty sub-boundaries `B' ⊑ B` or a lexically scoped `→I` hypothetical sub-proof box (without colliding with ambient `-A` or exploding), and one-way excluded (`-`) locally on `B` when `+A` and `-C` hold on `B` (`+A ∧ -C ⇒ -(A → C)`, barring inversion from `imposs-excl` into `+A` in `UL-Core`). | [`layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier`](design-review.md#layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier) | decided |
| **Stratified Reflection & Strict Positivity** | Curry Paradox barrier requiring reflection over level `n` (or frozen state+boundary snapshot `Σ_t`) to enter strictly at level `n+1` (or `Σ_{t+1}`), and prohibiting unguarded negative/implicational/boundary-promotion cycles in single and mutually recursive definitions. | [`layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier`](design-review.md#layer4-implication-and-curry-barrier) | decided |
| **Conjunction (`A ∧ C`)** | Dual-polarity conjunction on `B`: witnessed (`+`) iff both `+A` and `+C` hold unquarantined on `B` (joining provenance DAGs); excluded (`-`) iff `-A` or `-C` holds on `B`. | [`layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers`](design-review.md#layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers) | decided |
| **Cover-Witnessed Disjunction (`A ∨ C`) & Standalone Cover-Gluing** | Dual-polarity disjunction on `B` (`D2`): excluded (`-`) iff both `-A` and `-C` hold on `B`; witnessed (`+`) iff an explicit witnessed finite exhaustive cover `{B₁, ..., B_k} ▷ B` exists where each non-empty cell witnesses `+A` or `+C`. **Standalone Cover-Gluing** grounds `B ⊩± E` whenever every non-empty cell `B_i` of a witnessed cover holds `Live B_i ⊩± E` uniformly. | [`layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers`](design-review.md#layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers) | decided |
| **Witnessed Exhaustive Cover (`B = ⨆ B_i`)** | Explicit, structurally verified finite partition/cover of boundary `B` into sub-boundaries `{B₁, ..., B_k}` leaving no omitted cases; required to witness multi-cell disjunctions and case-analysis gluing. | [`layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers`](design-review.md#layer4-conjunction-disjunction-and-covers) | decided |
| **Role-Indexed Relational Claim (`R(r₁: t₁, ..., r_n: t_n)`)** | Atomic evaluable content expressing a relation, configuration, or transformation over role-labeled handles (unary properties `P(x)` are the 1-ary special case). | [`layer5-content-and-coreference`](design-review.md#layer5-content-and-coreference) | decided |
| **Boundary-Scoped Equivalence (`t₁ ≈_B t₂`) & Individuation (`I_B(t)`)** | Witnessed congruence relation (reflexive on admissible handles, symmetric, transitive, and substitutive over all relations native to `B`), paired with boundary-relative individuation criteria `I_B(t)` to prevent cross-update (`Σ₀ → Σ₁`) equivocation and allow multi-granularity coexistence. | [`layer5-content-and-coreference`](design-review.md#layer5-content-and-coreference) | decided |
| **Boundary-Scoped Quantifiers (`∀(x : B)` / `∃(x : B)`)** | Quantification bounded explicitly by a domain boundary `B` (with optional constraint `[δ]`) rather than a cosmic domain: `+∀` holds generically or via schematic `∀I` with strict eigenvariable freshness (`∀E` instantiates across admissible `[δ]`-satisfying handles); `+∃` requires a `Live` `[δ]`-witness (`∃E` enforces non-escape); `-∀` requires a concrete counterexample; `-∃` requires generic/constraint exclusion. | [`layer5-content-and-coreference`](design-review.md#layer5-content-and-coreference) | decided |
| **Structural Anti-Immunization (M1)** | Admissibility rule for boundary splitting `B' = B ⊓ [δ]` after counterexample `-A(w)`: requires (i) **Non-Circularity** (no reference to `A`), (ii) **Non-Contingent Structural Definability** (forbids bare contingent handle excision while permitting structurally defined singularities like `0` in `ℝ \ {0}`), and (iii) **Independent Excess-Content Witness** (when contingent handles are excised into `B'`, `B'` must stay non-empty with a `Live +δ(u)` witness on a non-refuted surviving handle and no surviving handle witnessing `Live -δ(v)`, subject to the `Formal Horizon Corollary`). | [`layer5-content-and-coreference`](design-review.md#layer5-content-and-coreference) | decided |
| **Specialization Hierarchy (`UL-Core` / `UL-Minimal` / `UL-Intuitionistic` / `UL-Classical`)** | Four-tier deductive spectrum derived solely via explicit closure conditions on `(B, Σ)` without conflicting rules: `UL-Core` (general) ⊃ `UL-Minimal` (monotone `Σ`, `No-Gluts`) ⊃ `UL-Intuitionistic` (`+ ⊥ → C`) ⊃ `UL-Classical` (frozen `Σ`, closed `B*`, `No-Gaps`). | [`layer2-classical-boundary-recovery`](design-review.md#layer2-classical-boundary-recovery) | decided |
| **No-Gaps / No-Gluts** | Explicit closure conditions on `(B, Σ)`: **No-Gaps** means no item is `Open` (every item has `+` or `-`); **No-Gluts** means no item is in `Collision`. Used to characterize classical and constructive boundary specializations. | [`scope-anchor-unified-core`](design-review.md#scope-anchor-unified-core) | decided |
