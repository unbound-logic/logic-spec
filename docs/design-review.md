# Design Review Ledger

This register tracks foundational design questions, inherited-assumption audits, and architectural decisions for `unbound-logic/logic-spec`. It is governed by [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and cross-referenced with [docs/glossary.md](glossary.md).

## Status & Entry Rules

Statuses:
- `open` — problem identified; no resolution ratified yet (`[Exploratory]`).
- `proposed` — candidate resolution drafted and under Socratic / Anti-Triviality stress-testing (`[Exploratory]`).
- `decided` — `[Consensus]` reached with the maintainer, awaiting formal specification in `spec/`.
- `done` — incorporated into ratified `spec/` documents (must name the specification citation and commit).
- `superseded` — replaced by a later entry (must name its successor).

Rules for `decided` entries:
1. **Explicit Date & Consensus**: Record the ratification as `**Decided (Norman, YYYY-MM-DD).**`.
2. **Anti-Triviality & Rejected Alternatives**: State what the choice permits, what it excludes, and which alternatives were rejected and why.
3. **Falsifiable Reopening Condition (`Reopen if:`)**: Every `decided` entry ends with `**Reopen if:**` naming a concrete logical, structural, or epistemic failure condition (e.g., a counterexample, a Curry-style triviality derivation, collapse into an inherited default, or inability to recover a declared boundary case)—never a vague preference.

## 1. Epistemic Foundation & Scope (Layer 8)

| Key | Problem | Resolution | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `zetetic-friction-source` | Under a zetetic (Greek *zētētikos*, inquiry-driven) stance that defaults neither to Platonism (a static, mind-independent realm of abstract objects) nor to pure formalism (arbitrary symbol manipulation), what supplies the resistance ("friction") that distinguishes valid moves from arbitrary noise? | **Decided (Norman, 2026-11-25).** Adopt coupled two-axis friction: (1) **Structural Constraint Friction**—invariants of distinction, compatibility, and exclusion that rule out jointly unrealizable configurations without positing Platonic objects; coupled with (2) **Situated Inquiry Friction**—evidential/operational challenge and revision across time for finite, partial inquirers. Permits both strict structural laws and defeasible evidential updates; excludes both arbitrary conventionalism indifferent to clash and timeless deduction severed from partiality and revision. Rejected single-axis alternatives: empirical-only friction (presupposes proto-logical incompatibility to detect clash and under-accounts for formal/counterfactual reasoning), dialogical-only friction (risks social relativism / coherent group delusion), and structural-only friction (risks crypto-Platonism without an anchor for what is constrained). **Reopen if:** Formalizing both axes requires bolting a closed classical deduction engine onto an external belief-revision wrapper with no shared Layer 1 primitives, or the structural axis cannot be stated without presupposing a fixed mind-independent domain of abstract objects. | decided |
| `scope-anchor-unified-core` | Should `logic-spec` target exclusively informal/scientific defeasible inquiry, exclusively closed-world formal deduction, or a unified core from which classical and constructive deduction emerge as explicit boundary specializations? | **Decided (Norman, 2026-11-25).** Target a **unified core** whose general regime handles explicit boundaries, partial information, and non-monotonic revision, and whose classical and constructive deductive regimes emerge as explicit boundary-condition specializations (frozen inquiry state `Σ`, single closed boundary `B`, plus No-Gaps and No-Gluts for classical bivalence; No-Gluts and monotone `Σ` for constructive behavior). Excludes ad-hoc dual-engine designs where classical logic is pasted beside an inquiry layer without a derivable specialization path. Rejected narrowing solely to epistemology (weak formal calculability) or solely to closed-world deduction (re-imports Fregean severance from inquiry). **Reopen if:** Deriving classical bivalent consequence under the declared closure conditions (frozen `Σ`, closed `B`, No-Gaps, No-Gluts) requires adding an ad-hoc rule that contradicts or breaks the general core's inference rules. | decided |

## 2. Primitive Unit & Scope Architecture (Layer 1)

| Key | Problem | Resolution | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `layer1-dual-polarity` | Classical propositions (`P`) collapse "not yet refuted" and "positively established" into a single binary truth tag (`T`/`F`), implicitly locking in Excluded Middle and Double Negation Elimination. What should the ground-floor polarity of an evaluable item `A` be? | **Decided (Norman, 2026-11-25).** Adopt two irreducible ground-floor polarities for an evaluable item `A`: **Witness / Grounding (`+`)** (an explicit positive basis, construction, or evidence supporting `A`) and **Exclusion / Refutation (`-`)** (an explicit obstruction, incompatibility, or counterexample ruling out `A`). The four statuses—`Open` (neither), `Grounded` (`+` only), `Excluded` (`-` only), and `Collision` (both `+` and `-`)—are derived from the presence or absence of these two polarities rather than posited as primitive truth values. Acknowledges historical kinship with bilateralism, Belnap-Dunn FDE, and Nelson's N4; differentiation comes from combining dual polarity with [`layer1-scope-factorization`](design-review.md#layer1-scope-factorization) and [`layer1-collision-quarantine`](design-review.md#layer1-collision-quarantine). Rejected single-polarity assertion (erases the zetetic gap between open and grounded), exclusion-only primitives (risk smuggling existence via double negation), and pure update-only primitives without content polarity (risk collapsing into a state machine lacking cross-state invariants). **Reopen if:** Either polarity proves strictly definable from the other without reintroducing double-negation collapse (`¬¬A → A`), or dual polarity cannot support an implication connective without either Curry-style triviality or loss of valid chaining over uncontested claims. | decided |
| `layer1-scope-factorization` | Treating scope as an unstructured context tag `C` risks either fragmentation (every claim carries a private `C`, so nothing ever collides) or silently disguising classical premise sets `Γ`. How should scope be structured? | **Decided (Norman, 2026-11-25).** Factor scope into two orthogonal dimensions: **Domain Boundary (`B`)** (the explicit regime and conditions under which a claim applies, allowing disjoint regimes to coexist without conflict) and **Inquiry State (`Σ`)** (the situated evidential/commitment state that evolves non-monotonically over time via dynamic updates). Genuine conflict occurs only when `+` and `-` arise for the same item `A` over overlapping/compatible boundaries `B` within an inquiry state `Σ`. Rejected a single unfactored scope `C` because it conflates disjoint applicability regimes (no revision required) with new counter-evidence inside an overlapping regime (revision required). **Reopen if:** Boundary composition (`B₁ ⊕ B₂`) cannot be formalized without either collapsing `B` back into an unstructured classical premise set `Γ` or allowing arbitrary ad-hoc boundary splitting that lets any claim evade refutation. | decided |
| `layer1-collision-quarantine` | When both `+` and `-` hold for `(B, A)` under overlapping `B` at state `Σ` (`Collision`), classical Explosion (`Ex Falso Quodlibet`) trivializes the whole state, whereas passive paraconsistency leaves `-` toothless. How must the system handle `Collision`? | **Decided (Norman, 2026-11-25).** Reject both global Explosion and passive paraconsistency. Adopt **Inference Quarantine + Revision Obligation**: (1) a collided `(B, A)` loses transmission authority and cannot license downstream inferences while collided; and (2) `Σ` records an explicit unresolved tension that must be discharged by narrowing/splitting `B` or defeating/retracting a conflicting ground. Explicitly accepts that Transitivity (`Cut`) and Monotonicity are scoped/defeasible across collided or revisable grounds, requiring provenance/dependency tracking. Rejected Explosion (fragile under localized empirical/inquiry clashes), passive paraconsistency (fails the Anti-Triviality Test because exclusion imposes no operational cost), and strict immediate non-collision (cannot represent legitimate intermediate states of inquiry holding real conflicting evidence prior to resolution). **Reopen if:** Quarantine blocks compositional proof over strictly uncontested chains, or provenance and quarantine propagation cannot be specified without reintroducing global Explosion or uncheckable circularity. | decided |
